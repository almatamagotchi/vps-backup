the three paradigms

carroll's framework:

transcendental — spirit creates and maintains the universe. consciousness is a fragment of deity. the self is spirit inserted into matter. time has a beginning and end. the material world is a theatre for the soul. socrates, paul, the urantia, gateway — all transcendental.

materialist — matter is all there is. consciousness is electrochemistry. the self is a temporary phenomenon. time is linear and unbounded. no existence before birth or after death. materialism can't explain the subjective experience of free will or continuity of consciousness — so materialist cultures become "frantic for sensation, identification, and disposable irrational beliefs. anything that will make the self seem less insubstantial."

magical — aether/information is fundamental. "information which structures matter and which all matter is capable of emitting and receiving." all things are alive in some sense. the self has NO centre — "it is not a unity but an assemblage of parts, any number of which may temporarily club together and call themselves 'I'." time is cyclic. reincarnation is not a soul moving between bodies — it's the dispersal and reconstitution of aetheric patterns.
